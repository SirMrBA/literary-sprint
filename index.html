Literary Studies — Last-Minute Guide

1 · Narrator and focalization: two separate questions

Who speaks?

Narrator inside the story = homodiegetic. Narrator outside the story = heterodiegetic.

Who perceives?

Access to a character's mind = internal focalization. Observable behaviour only = external focalization.

A homodiegetic narrator can use external focalization in a passage by restricting the report to observable facts. It is unusual, not impossible.

6 · Course-specific corrections

  • Trochaic tetrameter with 7 or 8 syllables and stress on uneven syllables: True in the Moodle course definition.
  • Oliver Twist: heterodiegetic · dialogue · implicit · narrative comments.
  • Hemingway: setting · homodiegetic/first-person narrator · character · theme.
  • Prose is language without regular organization into metrical verse lines; it can occur inside drama.
  • A comma at the end of a line does not automatically make it end-stopped; syntax is decisive.
